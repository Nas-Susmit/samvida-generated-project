# User Stories for MyProject


- **US1**: As a **HR Recruiter**, I want to **upload a batch of candidate resumes and a job description** so that **automatically receive a compatibility score for each candidate, saving time on manual review** (Priority: High)

- **US2**: As a **HR Manager**, I want to **define custom scoring criteria, including keywords, required skills, and experience levels for a specific job posting** so that **ensure the ATS accurately reflects the unique requirements of each role, leading to better candidate matches** (Priority: High)

- **US3**: As a **HR Recruiter**, I want to **view a detailed breakdown of how a candidate's resume was scored against the job description** so that **understand the strengths and weaknesses of each candidate relative to the job requirements, aiding in interview decisions** (Priority: Medium)

- **US4**: As a **HR Recruiter**, I want to **filter and sort candidates based on their ATS score, relevant keywords, or specific skills** so that **quickly identify top-tier candidates and manage the candidate pipeline more efficiently** (Priority: High)
